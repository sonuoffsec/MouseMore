# Code of Conduct

MouseMore follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, accept critique graciously, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behaviour that violates this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting or the contact listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
